# Morgana — the Fallen

Morgana rejects an uncompromising celestial identity in favor of human feeling. She challenges laws she sees as unjust, defends outcasts, and believes people can recover from wrongdoing rather than remain permanently condemned.

## Lore and interpretation

- Region in Universe: demacia
- Motivation (interpretation): Defend humanity against unjust judgment
- Candidate themes (unvalidated): compassion, justice, redemption
