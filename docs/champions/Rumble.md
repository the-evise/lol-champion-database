# Rumble — the Mechanized Menace

Rumble builds formidable machines from discarded materials. His temper and pride accompany a practical inventiveness that answers others' dismissal with a working combat mech rather than deference to established prestige.

## Lore and interpretation

- Region in Universe: bandle-city
- Motivation (interpretation): Prove the worth of his own inventions
- Candidate themes (unvalidated): craft, defiance, pride
