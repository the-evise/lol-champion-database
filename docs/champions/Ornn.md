# Ornn — The Fire below the Mountain

Ornn is a reclusive Freljordian forge deity devoted to making things of exceptional quality. He prefers his underground workshop, but intervenes when other divine beings disrupt mortal life or overstep their influence.

## Lore and interpretation

- Region in Universe: freljord
- Motivation (interpretation): Practice his craft and restrain divine interference
- Candidate themes (unvalidated): craft, independence, restraint
