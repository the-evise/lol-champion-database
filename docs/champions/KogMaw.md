# Kog'Maw — the Mouth of the Abyss

Kog'Maw is a curious Void creature that learns by biting and dissolving whatever it finds. Its naive interest in the world is dangerous because exploration easily becomes indiscriminate feeding.

## Lore and interpretation

- Region in Universe: void
- Motivation (interpretation): Understand the world through consumption
- Candidate themes (unvalidated): curiosity, hunger, innocence
