# Thresh — the Chain Warden

Thresh imprisons souls and studies ways to prolong their suffering. His former custodianship of secrets has become an undead practice of manipulation and deliberate cruelty, with his lantern preserving victims beyond bodily death.

## Lore and interpretation

- Region in Universe: shadow-isles
- Motivation (interpretation): Control and torment captured souls
- Candidate themes (unvalidated): control, cruelty, manipulation
