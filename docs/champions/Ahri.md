# Ahri — the Nine-Tailed Fox

A vastaya who absorbs emotions and memories through consumed life essence, Ahri is moving beyond her predatory past. She travels to understand her ancestry and build experiences that belong to her rather than to her victims.

- Region in Universe: ionia
