# Kled — the Cantankerous Cavalier

Kled is a combative Noxian yordle whose military legend is difficult to separate from exaggeration. Riding Skaarl, he fiercely defends what he considers his territory while trying to take more.

## Lore and interpretation

- Region in Universe: noxus
- Motivation (interpretation): Defend and expand what he considers his
- Candidate themes (unvalidated): defiance, territoriality, audacity
