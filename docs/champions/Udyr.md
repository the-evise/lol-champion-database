# Udyr — the Spirit Walker

Udyr experiences and channels the spirits of the Freljord. He works to retain his own identity within their voices while pursuing a regional balance that, in his view, sometimes requires conflict rather than uninterrupted peace.

## Lore and interpretation

- Region in Universe: freljord
- Motivation (interpretation): Balance spiritual forces within and around him
- Candidate themes (unvalidated): balance, empathy, inner-conflict
