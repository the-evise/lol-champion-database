# Zed — the Master of Shadows

Zed founded the Order of Shadow to turn Ionian traditions into a force against invasion. He embraces dangerous forbidden magic and ruthless methods to eliminate threats to his homeland and to the organization he leads.

- Region in Universe: ionia
