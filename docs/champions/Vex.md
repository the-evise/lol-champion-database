# Vex — the Gloomist

Vex chooses isolation in the Shadow Isles with her powerful shadow. Rather than seek a large ambition, she protects her gloomy refuge and reacts strongly against the cheerfulness and color she finds intrusive.

## Lore and interpretation

- Region in Universe: shadow-isles
- Motivation (interpretation): Preserve her refuge from unwanted cheer
- Candidate themes (unvalidated): withdrawal, defiance, gloom
