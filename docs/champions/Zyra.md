# Zyra — Rise of the Thorns

Zyra arose from a magical catastrophe as a union of plant and human form. She spreads predatory vegetation and treats other life as material for its growth, although the source leaves her ultimate purpose unresolved.

## Lore and interpretation

- Region in Universe: ixtal
- Motivation (interpretation): Spread her growth; ultimate purpose unclear
