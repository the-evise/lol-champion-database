# Singed — the Mad Chemist

Singed sells exceptional alchemical skill with little concern for its victims. His work on shimmer helped transform Zaun's power structures, and the destructive results of experiments become further material for his continuing research.

## Lore and interpretation

- Region in Universe: zaun
- Motivation (interpretation): Pursue experimentation without moral restraint
- Candidate themes (unvalidated): curiosity, detachment, obsession
