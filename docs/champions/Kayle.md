# Kayle — the Righteous

Kayle inherited celestial power and once protected Demacia alongside Morgana. Disillusionment with human failings led her away from the mortal world, while her reputation remains one of severe divine judgment.

## Lore and interpretation

- Region in Universe: demacia
- Motivation (interpretation): Enforce her vision of justice
