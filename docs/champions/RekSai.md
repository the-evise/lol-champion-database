# Rek'Sai — the Void Burrower

Rek'Sai hunts beneath Shurima, using tunnels to ambush prey. Her feeding and offspring make large areas dangerous to travelers, forcing people to avoid territory shaped around an apex predator's needs.

## Lore and interpretation

- Region in Universe: void
- Motivation (interpretation): Hunt and feed across her territory
