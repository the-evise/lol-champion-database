# Galio — the Colossus

Galio is a magical stone defender who awakens in the presence of strong magic. He relishes protecting Demacians, but destroying the magic that animates him inevitably returns him to immobility.

## Lore and interpretation

- Region in Universe: demacia
- Motivation (interpretation): Protect Demacians during his brief awakenings
- Candidate themes (unvalidated): protection, joy, sacrifice
