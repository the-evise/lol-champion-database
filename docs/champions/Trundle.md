# Trundle — the Troll King

Trundle defends his domain with cunning and overwhelming violence. His True Ice club and control of frozen terrain support a territorial attitude in which intruders become targets rather than potential companions.

## Lore and interpretation

- Region in Universe: freljord
- Motivation (interpretation): Dominate and defend his territory
