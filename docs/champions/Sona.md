# Sona — Maven of the Strings

Sona communicates through the music of her etwahl and is celebrated among Demacian nobles. The possibility that her performances are magical complicates that acceptance, while her music can both protect allies and harm enemies.

## Lore and interpretation

- Region in Universe: demacia
- Motivation (interpretation): Connect and protect through music
