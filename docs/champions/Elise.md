# Elise — the Spider Queen

Once a Noxian noblewoman, Elise became a spiderlike predator after encountering a spider god. She uses an attractive human appearance to draw victims close and sustain her transformed existence.

- Region in Universe: shadow-isles
- Motivation (interpretation): Sustain herself through predation
