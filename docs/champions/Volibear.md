# Volibear — the Relentless Storm

Volibear regards civilization as a weakening of the Freljord's original wildness. The storm deity seeks a return to older ways through destruction, fiercely resisting people or gods who oppose his vision of the land.

## Lore and interpretation

- Region in Universe: freljord
- Motivation (interpretation): Restore a wild and violent Freljord
- Candidate themes (unvalidated): tradition, domination, defiance
