# Darius — the Hand of Noxus

Darius rose from modest beginnings to lead Noxus's Trifarian Legion. His certainty in the empire's cause supports uncompromising military action, including against fellow Noxians who stand in its way.

## Lore and interpretation

- Region in Universe: noxus
- Motivation (interpretation): Advance Noxus through military strength
