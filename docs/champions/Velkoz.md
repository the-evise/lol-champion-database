# Vel'Koz — the Eye of the Void

Vel'Koz studies the physical world to discover weaknesses the Void can exploit. Its curiosity is analytical and destructive: the beings it investigates are objects of research rather than lives it feels obliged to preserve.

## Lore and interpretation

- Region in Universe: void
- Motivation (interpretation): Acquire knowledge useful to the Void
- Candidate themes (unvalidated): analysis, curiosity, detachment
