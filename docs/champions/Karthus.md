# Karthus — the Deathsinger

Karthus embraces undeath as a beautiful resolution rather than a horror. His travels from the Shadow Isles are driven by a missionary desire to bring mortals into the condition he celebrates.

## Lore and interpretation

- Region in Universe: shadow-isles
- Motivation (interpretation): Bring others into his vision of undeath
- Candidate themes (unvalidated): devotion, transcendence, fanaticism
