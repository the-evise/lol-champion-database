# Dr. Mundo — the Madman of Zaun

A former asylum patient, Mundo now imitates the abusive medical practices once used on him. He believes himself a doctor despite lacking medical understanding, and his experiments make him more dangerous to everyone around him.

## Lore and interpretation

- Region in Universe: zaun
- Motivation (interpretation): Practice his distorted idea of medicine
- Candidate themes (unvalidated): delusion, imitation, harm
