# Tryndamere — the Barbarian King

The destruction of his clan drove Tryndamere toward revenge and relentless combat. His marriage alliance with Ashe adds companionship and a wider political purpose to the strength and endurance that made him feared.

## Lore and interpretation

- Region in Universe: freljord
