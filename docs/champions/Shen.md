# Shen — the Eye of Twilight

Shen leads the Kinkou and seeks judgment unclouded by personal feeling. His task is to preserve equilibrium between the physical and spirit worlds, even when doing so conflicts with ordinary loyalties or emotional reactions.

## Lore and interpretation

- Region in Universe: ionia
- Motivation (interpretation): Maintain equilibrium through impartial judgment
