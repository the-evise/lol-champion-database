# Zoe — the Aspect of Twilight

Zoe serves as Targon's cosmic messenger while approaching existence as an opportunity for play. Her extraordinary effect on reality can cause disaster without hostile intent, creating a mismatch between her lighthearted behavior and its consequences.

## Lore and interpretation

- Region in Universe: mount-targon
- Motivation (interpretation): Carry cosmic messages while seeking amusement
- Candidate themes (unvalidated): playfulness, imagination, unpredictability
