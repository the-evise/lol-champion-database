# Kalista — the Spear of Vengeance

Kalista answers calls for vengeance from people betrayed by others. Her supernatural bargains demand the petitioner's soul, turning the pursuit of retribution into an irreversible commitment with a terrible personal cost.

## Lore and interpretation

- Region in Universe: shadow-isles
- Motivation (interpretation): Punish betrayal through binding vengeance
- Candidate themes (unvalidated): loyalty, vengeance, absolutism
