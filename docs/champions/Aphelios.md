# Aphelios — the Weapon of the Faithful

Aphelios fights for the Lunari using weapons supplied through his sister Alune's distant sanctuary. A ritual poison prevents him from speaking while enabling their connection, making his solitary missions dependent on a close spiritual partnership.

## Lore and interpretation
