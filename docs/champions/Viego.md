# Viego — The Ruined King

Viego's attempt to resurrect his wife caused the Ruination. In undeath he remains fixated on bringing her back, directing the Black Mist through the world and accepting devastation as a consequence of his pursuit.

## Lore and interpretation

- Region in Universe: shadow-isles
- Motivation (interpretation): Recover his lost wife regardless of cost
- Candidate themes (unvalidated): attachment, obsession, denial
