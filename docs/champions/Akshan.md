# Akshan — the Rogue Sentinel

Akshan is a vigilante whose fighting style combines stealth with conspicuous bravado. Carrying a weapon capable of reversing deaths, he pursues wrongdoers according to a personal sense of justice rather than formal authority.

## Lore and interpretation

- Region in Universe: shurima
- Motivation (interpretation): Correct wrongs through personal justice
- Candidate themes (unvalidated): justice, audacity, independence
