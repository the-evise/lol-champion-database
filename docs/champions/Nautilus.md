# Nautilus — the Titan of the Depths

Bilgewater legends describe Nautilus as an armored figure driven by betrayal beneath the sea. He punishes greed and broken maritime obligations, with sailors connecting his attacks to failure to pay the ocean's tithe.

## Lore and interpretation

- Region in Universe: bilgewater
- Motivation (interpretation): Punish betrayal and unpaid debts
- Candidate themes (unvalidated): vengeance, obligation, judgment
