# Twitch — the Plague Rat

Twitch is a mutated Zaunite rat who embraces the filth others reject. He scavenges discarded objects and directs hostility toward Piltover, intending to expose the corruption beneath its polished image.

## Lore and interpretation

- Region in Universe: zaun
- Motivation (interpretation): Expose Piltover's filth on his own terms
