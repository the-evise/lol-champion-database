# Jhin — the Virtuoso

Jhin treats carefully arranged murders as artistic performances. Released from imprisonment by covert Ionian patrons, he works as an assassin whose staged violence is intended to terrify audiences as much as kill its targets.

## Lore and interpretation

- Region in Universe: ionia
- Motivation (interpretation): Realize his violent artistic vision
