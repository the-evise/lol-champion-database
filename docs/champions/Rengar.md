# Rengar — the Pridestalker

Rengar hunts dangerous creatures for the satisfaction of the pursuit rather than basic need or public acclaim. His rivalry with Kha'Zix, who injured him, gives that search for challenging prey a particular focus.

- Region in Universe: ixtal
